Getting started

Start SnapForge from the Start menu (installer) or run SnapForge.exe (portable ZIP). On the first start a short welcome explains the basics; reopen it with Help → Getting started. The capture panel opens, and a small widget tab appears at the top centre of your screen. SnapForge also puts an icon in the notification area (next to the clock); right-click it for the app's menu, including Exit.

SnapForge has three workspaces: Capture (the panel), the Editor and the Library. Switch with the buttons in the top-right corner or with Ctrl+E and Ctrl+L.

Taking a capture

  1. In the capture panel, pick a Selection: Region, Window, Active window, Display or Full screen.
  2. Click the big Capture button or press Print Screen from any app.
  3. For a region, drag a rectangle over the frozen screen. For a window or display, click it. The magnifier shows the exact pixel; arrow keys move the selection by one pixel (Shift for ten). Press Esc or right-click to cancel.

Capture → Repeat last capture (Ctrl+R) captures the same area again; New from clipboard (Ctrl+Shift+V) opens a copied image as a capture.

Options in the panel: Preview in Editor, Copy to Clipboard, Save to Library (turn off to capture without keeping a copy), Cursor and Time Delay. Save combinations as Presets, each with its own hotkey.

Annotating

The Editor toolbar has Select (V), Arrow (A), Line (L), Shape/Rectangle (R), Ellipse (E), Highlight (H), Text (T), Step (N), Pen (D), Callout (K), Stamp (S), Redact (X), Blur (B), Pixelate (P) and Crop (C). The Image menu rotates, flips, resizes and changes the canvas size, and adds a border, drop shadow or watermark.

  • Hold Shift while drawing to keep lines straight and shapes square.
  • Click an annotation to select it; drag to move, use the handles to resize, double-click text to edit, Del to remove.
  • Pick colour and thickness from Quick Styles; Save current adds your own, and right-click marks a favourite.
  • Copy, paste and duplicate annotations with Ctrl+Shift+C, Ctrl+V and Ctrl+D.
  • Undo with Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Y.

Redacting

Choose Redact (X) and drag over anything private. The area becomes solid black.

What is removed, and where. Images you export, copy or drag out contain only the black box. The editable copy in your Library still keeps the original pixels so you can undo the redaction later; the Editor shows a banner while that is the case. Choose Sanitize (in the banner, or in the Library) to remove the originals for good. Blur and pixelate are not redaction. Videos cannot be redacted.

Saving and sharing

  • Copy (Ctrl+C) puts the finished image on the clipboard.
  • Export (Ctrl+S) saves PNG, JPEG, BMP or TIFF; Print is Ctrl+P.
  • Drag: use the Drag button in the Editor, or drag a thumbnail from the recent captures tray or the Library, into an email, chat, document or folder. Settings → Drag and drop chooses PNG or JPEG.

The Library

Every capture is saved to the Library and stays editable. Use the sidebar for All Captures, Recent, Favorites, Tags, Folders and Trash; search and sort above the thumbnails. Double-click or press Enter to open a capture, F2 to rename, Del to move it to the Trash, and right-click for more. Settings has backup and restore, retention, storage usage and the Library location.

The desktop widget

Hover over (or click, depending on Settings) the tab at the top of the screen to open Capture, Editor, Library and Presets. Drag it by its grip to the top, left or right edge of any display. If you lose it, choose View → Show capture widget. It never takes keyboard focus and hides itself during a capture.

Scrolling capture and text

Capture → Scrolling capture (Ctrl+6): select the part of the window that scrolls, then choose Auto-scroll or scroll yourself and choose Add frame after each step, then Finish. If the app redraws while scrolling and the pieces cannot be matched exactly, SnapForge tells you instead of guessing — scroll a little less at a time.

Image → Extract text (Ctrl+Shift+T) reads the text in a capture with Windows' built-in, offline text recognition. Check and edit the text, then copy it. If no recognition language is installed, add one in Windows Settings → Time & language → Language & region.

Screen recording

In the capture panel choose Video, pick region, window or display, then Record (or Shift+F9). After a 3-second countdown, a floating toolbar shows the elapsed time with Pause/Resume, Stop and Cancel, and switches for system sound and microphone. Shift+F9 pauses and resumes; Shift+F10 stops and saves an MP4 to the Library. Windows shows a border around what is being recorded.

Editing videos

In the Library, right-click a video and choose Edit video. Set the start and end with [ and ], mark sections to remove with M, and step frame by frame with , and .. Save a frame to the Library as an image, save the edited video to the Library, or export it as MP4 or as an animated GIF. Edited videos are re-encoded; GIFs have no sound.

Where your data lives

  • Library: %LOCALAPPDATA%\SnapForge\Library, unless you choose another folder in Settings.
  • Settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\SnapForge\settings.json.
  • Error logs (text only, never images): %LOCALAPPDATA%\SnapForge\logs.
  • Temporary copies for drag-and-drop: removed at once when a drag is cancelled; after a drop they are kept up to 24 hours so slow uploads finish. Settings → Delete drag-and-drop copies now removes them.

Library backups (.zip) contain images only; back up the Library's videos folder separately.
