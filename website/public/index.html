What it does

From screen to shared in a few seconds

SnapForge is being built in the open. Each feature below says how far along it is — what the labels mean.

Development build

Screenshot capture

Region, window, single display or full screen, across several monitors. A delay timer, the mouse pointer on or off, and presets with their own hotkeys.

Development build

Annotations

Arrows, lines, boxes, ellipses, highlights, text and numbered steps, with Quick Styles and full undo/redo. Crop to what matters.

Development build

Redaction that sticks

Redact replaces pixels with solid black. Exported and copied images are flattened, so there is nothing hidden underneath to recover. How it works

Development build

Library

Every capture is saved automatically and stays editable: favourites, tags, folders, search, Trash and backup — all stored locally on your PC.

Release candidate 2 (in testing)

QuickDock

A compact bar at the edge of the screen (provisional name, new original design) opens Capture, Record, Editor, Library and your presets without switching windows. Dock it to the top, left or right.

Development build

Drag and drop

Drag a capture from the Editor, the Library or the recent captures tray straight into another app as a normal image file.

Release candidate 2 (in testing)

SnapForge Studio

Record your screen, your screen with your camera, or just your camera, to MP4 (provisional name). Camera and microphone are off until you switch them on, and everything stays on your PC. Not approved for release yet.

Local by design

The app has no network code: no uploads, sync, accounts, update checks or telemetry. Your captures stay on your PC. Privacy details
