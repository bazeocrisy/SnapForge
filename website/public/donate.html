Donate

Support SnapForge

SnapForge is free and open source. If it makes your day easier, consider supporting its continued development.

Donation options are not set up yet. They will appear here once the owner has created and verified them. Nothing on this site asks for payment details.

Good to know

  • Donations are entirely optional. Every feature is free for everyone; nothing is locked behind a payment.
  • Donations are not tax-deductible and are not payment for a service, support contract or priority.
  • Payments are handled entirely by the provider you choose, on its own website and under its own terms and privacy policy. This site embeds no payment forms or scripts.

Other ways to help

  • Test a release on your PC and report what you find.
  • Suggest improvements to the documentation or the user guide.
  • Tell people who might find SnapForge useful.
