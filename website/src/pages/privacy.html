Privacy

Plain-language summary of what the SnapForge app and this website do with your information. Last updated 27 November 2026.

The SnapForge app

  • No network access. The app contains no code that connects to the internet: no uploads, sync, accounts, update checks, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • Local storage. Captures, recordings (including camera recordings) and settings are stored on your PC in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SnapForge (or a Library folder you choose). Error logs contain text only — never image data — and stay on your PC.
  • Not encrypted by SnapForge. Your files are protected by your Windows account; use Windows device encryption or BitLocker to protect the disk.
  • Redaction. Exported, copied and dragged images are flattened, with redacted areas burned in and no metadata. Your editable Library copy keeps original pixels until you choose Sanitize. Videos cannot be redacted.
  • Sharing is your choice. When you paste, drag or export a capture into another app, that app keeps its own copy under its own privacy terms.
  • Recording with SnapForge Studio (release candidate 2, in testing) uses Windows' screen capture, camera and audio APIs. Windows shows a border while the screen is recorded, its microphone indicator while the microphone is on and its camera indicator while the camera is on. SnapForge does not hide or bypass these indicators.
  • Camera. The camera and the microphone are off by default and are switched on only by you, for a recording. The camera picture is processed only on your PC (through Windows' own camera support) and is saved only into the recording you make, in your Library. It is never uploaded, streamed or analysed. Windows' privacy settings (Settings → Privacy & security → Camera / Microphone) control whether desktop apps may use them at all.
  • Backups you create contain your captures, recordings, Trash and their metadata. Store them as carefully as the Library itself.
  • Uninstalling keeps your data. Removing the app does not delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\SnapForge; delete it yourself to remove captures and settings.

This website

  • No cookies, analytics, advertising, fonts or scripts from other companies. The only script is a small local one for the mobile menu and the “copy checksum” button; it stores nothing.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, receives the technical information every website receives (such as your IP address and browser) and may keep logs under its privacy statement (external site). We do not receive or use those logs.
  • There are no forms, and the site never asks for personal or payment information.

Links to other websites

Links to GitHub, Microsoft documentation and — once set up — donation providers take you to sites with their own privacy policies. Links that leave this site are marked for screen readers as external.
