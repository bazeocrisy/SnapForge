Roadmap

Where SnapForge is heading

Plans can change. Nothing below is a promise of a date; each step is reviewed and approved before it ships.

Now: review and real-PC testing

The screenshot workflow (capture, Editor, Library, widget, drag-and-drop) is complete in development builds and waiting for independent review and testing on physical Windows 11 PCs. After approval, the first public release will be published on GitHub Releases and linked from the Download page.

Screen recording Experimental

Built and under review on its own branch. It needs testing with real sound devices, hardware video encoders, high-resolution displays, multiple monitors, sleep and lock before it can be approved.
